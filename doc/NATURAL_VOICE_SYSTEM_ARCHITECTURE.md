# Natural Neural Voice & Voice Copilot Architecture

ClauseGraph incorporates an **Enterprise-grade, Zero-Cost Natural Voice Intelligence System** built to provide high-clarity auditory comprehension for general counsels, risk officers, and enterprise executives without requiring recurring paid voice APIs (such as ElevenLabs).

---

## 1. Zero-Cost Natural Voice Hierarchy

The engine utilizes modern 2026 browser audio capabilities, prioritizing **Microsoft Online Natural Neural Voices** which are bundled with Edge/Chromium on Windows and macOS:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            Voice Selection Waterfall                              |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. Microsoft Aria Online (Natural) - Neural broadcast grade (Warm Executive)      |
|  2. Microsoft Jenny Online (Natural) - Studio grade clarity                       |
|  3. Microsoft Guy Online (Natural) - Resonant authoritative male voice           |
|  4. Google US English (Natural Chromium TTS)                                      |
|  5. System Default Fallback Voice                                                 |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Key Advantages:
- **Zero API Costs**: No per-character subscription or third-party quota limit.
- **Ultra-low latency**: Instant local streaming without round-trip audio file transfers.
- **Natural inflection**: High-fidelity pitch variation and natural pause cadence.

## 2. Voice Sanitization & Audio Chunking Engine

### A. Legal Text Sanitization (`sanitizeLegalTextForSpeech`)
Raw legal text contains markdown symbols, citations, asterisks, brackets, and raw currency strings that sound unnatural when read verbatim. Our engine converts these before speech:
- `**Bold**` / `*Italics*` -> Plain readable text
- `[Citation](url)` -> Citation label only
- `$450,000 USD` -> *"450 thousand US dollars"*
- Acronyms expanded for pronunciation:
  - `DORA` -> *"Dora, Digital Operational Resilience Act"*
  - `GDPR` -> *"General Data Protection Regulation"*
  - `MSA` -> *"Master Services Agreement"*
  - `DPA` -> *"Data Processing Addendum"*

### B. Sentence Chunking (`chunkTextIntoSentences`)
Web Speech API implementations in Chromium can encounter timeout halts on utterances longer than 15 seconds. ClauseGraph splits executive summaries and notification bodies into natural sentence boundaries (`.`, `!`, `?`, `;`) and plays them sequentially through an active queue with synchronized state tracking.

## 3. Executive Morning Compliance Briefing (Podcast Card)

Mounted at the top of the Enterprise Command Center:
- **Audio Length**: ~45 seconds.
- **Dynamic Content**:
  1. Total monitored portfolio valuation ($66.9M across 9 enterprise agreements).
  2. Urgent statutory shocks (Bangladesh Cyber Security Act 2023 repeal of DSA 2018; EU DORA Article 30 subcontracting candidate updates).
  3. Action items & pending human verification count in the Review Queue.
- **Interactive Controls**:
  - `Listen to Briefing` / `Pause` / `Resume` toggle.
  - Collapsible **Transcript Drawer** allowing users to read along in real time.

---

## 4. Notification Bell Voice Narration

Inside the topbar Notification Center:
- Every notification card features an interactive `Listen` button (`Volume2` icon).
- When clicked:
  - Converts into `Reading...` with active pulse animation.
  - Spawns the floating **Holographic Audio Waveform Player** at the bottom of the screen.
  - Narrates the notification title, jurisdiction badge, and plain-English risk advisory.

---

## 5. Voice Copilot (STT Dictation & Read Aloud)

The ClauseGraph AI Copilot features two-way voice integration:
- **Speech-to-Text (Mic Button)**: Users can click the microphone icon in the chat input to dictate prompts naturally (using Web Speech `SpeechRecognition` or Groq Whisper). The input box automatically populates with live transcribed text.
- **Read Aloud Speaker**: Every response generated by Groq LPU inference includes a volume icon to read aloud the legal synthesis with neural voice pacing.

## 6. Holographic Obsidian-Mint Waveform Visualizer

While audio is actively playing:
- A floating player docks at the bottom of the viewport (`AudioWaveform.tsx`).
- Renders **4 animated equalizer frequency bars** that dynamically dance during playback.
- Displays the active persona badge (e.g., `Aria Neural`).
- Provides 1-click **Pause / Play** and **Stop** controls.
